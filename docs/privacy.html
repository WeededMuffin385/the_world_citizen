Privacy Policy

The World Citizen

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The World Citizen Android application (package name: dev.zagoruiko.theworldcitizen) handles information. The application is developed and operated by Mikhail Zagoruiko ("I", "me", or "the developer"). This policy covers the current app, which lets you explore sample trips, view maps and daily itineraries, like trips, and select an active journey.

1. Information handled on your device

The app processes the following information locally to provide its features:

  • Search and trip preferences: search text, selected countries, travel dates, duration, and travel styles, used to filter sample trips.
  • Trip selections: liked trips, the active journey, and the itinerary day you choose to view, used to display your selections.
  • Display preferences: your theme selection and the device's light or dark appearance setting, used to display the interface.
  • Sample plan selection: the plan you select on the Account screen. This is a local demonstration feature; it does not create a subscription or process a payment.

These selections are kept in app memory during the current session. The app does not upload them to the developer, create a cloud profile, or synchronize them between devices. They reset when the app process is closed and restarted; simply switching away from the app may leave the process running.

2. Online maps and information sent to OpenStreetMap

The app uses MapLibre to display maps and requests street-map images (tiles) over HTTPS from tile.openstreetmap.org, a service operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Requests occur when a trip map is displayed, including when a map is loaded in a background tab, and as you move or zoom the map.

To deliver these tiles, OpenStreetMap and its delivery infrastructure receive your IP address, the requested map tile coordinates and zoom level, request times, and technical request metadata such as app or device information supplied by the networking software. An IP address can indicate an approximate geographic area. Tile requests reveal the area of the map being displayed; they do not contain your search text, liked-trip list, or travel dates.

This information is used by the provider to deliver maps and operate and protect its services. The developer does not receive a copy of these tile-request logs. OpenStreetMap's processing, service providers, retention practices, and international server infrastructure are described in the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. The app does not require an OpenStreetMap account.

3. Device location and sensitive information

The current app does not request or access your device's GPS or Android location permissions. Destinations and map markers are based on sample itineraries, not your physical location. The Android configuration blocks coarse and fine location permissions.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, SMS, call logs, or health information. It does not ask for your name, email address, password, identification documents, or payment details to use its trip features. The Account screen does not provide account registration or sign-in.

4. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

The current app does not integrate advertising, behavioral analytics, or a developer-operated crash-reporting service. It does not use an advertising identifier, sell personal information, or share your trip selections for marketing. The online map requests described above are necessary to display map imagery.

Google Play and Android may separately process installation, purchase-history, or diagnostic information under your Google account and device settings. Their independent processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Contacting the developer

If you email me, I receive your email address and the contents of your message, including any information or attachments you choose to provide. I use this information to respond to your question, resolve your request, and maintain necessary support correspondence. Email providers process these messages to deliver and store the correspondence. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Session selections: these remain in app memory until the process ends. You can unlike a trip using its heart button, reset Explore filters, or end the app process to clear session selections. Starting another trip replaces the active journey.
  • Device caches: map imagery or other resources may be cached by the map library or operating system. To remove app-managed local data and caches, use Android Settings to clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Developer-held trip data: the developer does not maintain a server-side database of your searches, favorites, or journeys. There is no app account to delete.
  • Support correspondence: I keep correspondence only for as long as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. If information must be retained for a legal obligation or dispute, I will explain the relevant limitation when responding.
  • Map-service logs: retention and deletion are controlled by OpenStreetMap under its privacy policy. Clearing app data does not delete records held by that provider; contact it through the channels in its policy for requests concerning those records.

7. Security

The app's map requests use HTTPS to protect information in transit. Trip selections stay in app memory rather than being sent to a developer-operated server. Device security and access controls help protect local information. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee complete security.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may use the local trip-selection features without providing an identity or creating an account. Disabling network access prevents new online map requests, although maps may then be unavailable. Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information, or complain to a relevant privacy authority. Contact me to exercise rights concerning information I hold. For information processed by a separate provider, use that provider's privacy contact.

9. Children's privacy

The current app does not include child-specific profiles or features that request children's personal information. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to me in support correspondence, please contact me so I can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Where required, material changes will also be communicated in the app and any necessary consent obtained before new processing begins.
